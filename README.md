# Open Decentralized UAP Research Tracker

An evolving, cross-referenced database of open-source, decentralized, and 
citizen-science UAP (Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena) research groups — 
tracking who's building real field equipment, what that equipment actually 
does, and how well their claims hold up under scrutiny.

**Live site:** https://mvwindsor.github.io/Open-Decentralized-UAP-Research-Tracker/

## What this is

This project catalogs UAP research groups with a specific focus on 
**hardware and data analysis** — actual sensors, actual field deployments, 
actual instrument specifications — rather than speculation about the nature 
of the phenomenon itself. It's organized into three sections:

- **Groups Database** — individual research organizations, their equipment, 
  methodology, and personnel, cross-referenced against each other wherever 
  their work overlaps
- **Equipment Index** — general-purpose sensor techniques and instruments, 
  not yet tied to a specific group's confirmed hardware
- **Technical Challenges** — documented methodological and physics-level 
  problems (sensor limitations, unresolved assumptions, open engineering 
  questions) that apply across multiple groups

## Sourcing standards

Every entry is checked against available primary sources before being 
logged. Where a claim can't be independently verified, that's stated 
explicitly rather than presented as settled fact. Where two sources 
conflict, both are noted rather than silently picking one. Nothing here 
should be read as a final verdict — it's a working record, updated as new 
information comes in.

## Status

This is an actively evolving project, not a finished product. Entries get 
corrected, expanded, and occasionally restructured as better information 
surfaces. Treat anything here as current as of its last update, not as a 
live feed.
